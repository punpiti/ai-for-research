## ข้อความในประกาศ

> Following the publication of this article [1], concerns were raised regarding the references. Specifically, References 2, 9-13, and 15 could not be retrieved. Upon editorial follow-up, the corresponding author requested retraction of the article. In light of the issue with the references that raises concerns about the integrity of the published article, the PLOS Neglected Tropical Diseases Editors retract this article. NKR and SKR agreed with the retraction. CT either did not respond directly or could not be reached.
