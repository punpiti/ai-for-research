## ข้อความในประกาศ

> This article has been retracted at the request of the Editors. Post-publication, an investigation conducted by Elsevier's Research Integrity & Publishing Ethics team on behalf of the journal identified references that are irrelevant to the article and a distinct lack of citations in large sections of the text. The author was asked to comment upon the references in their work but was unable to satisfactorily address the reason for the references. Additionally, there are concerns that the author appears to have used a Generative AI source in the writing process of the paper without disclosure, which is a breach of journal policy. Consequently, the editor no longer has confidence in the integrity and the findings of the article and has decided to retract it. The author disagrees with retraction and disputes the grounds for it.
