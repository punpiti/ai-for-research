## ข้อความในประกาศ

> The Editor has retracted this article after identifying multiple incorrect and unverifiable references (13, 7, 6, 4, 15 and 53). Some cited works could not be confirmed as existing publications (5, 12, 16, 47 and 48). Also, References 9 and 11 appear to be duplicates of 49 and 53, respectively. The Editor has lost confidence in the data and conclusions of this article. Ukpai Eze disagrees with the retraction. David Agom did not explicitly state whether or not they agree with this retraction. None of the remaining authors have responded to the correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction.
